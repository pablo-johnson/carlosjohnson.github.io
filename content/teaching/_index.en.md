Teaching is not a secondary activity for Carlos Johnson but the natural continuation of his work as a performer. It is split between two very different realities: the European student elite who come to Lübeck from all over the world, and the new generation of players in Peru.

At the centre of his teaching is respect for the composer: the performer is not a creator, but the person who brings an idea written by someone else back to life. Hence his insistence on discipline, on analysing the score, and on putting the dignity of the craft before the immediate appeal of fame.
